What each flag does

FlagEffectStatus
hide_speed=trueRemoves the playback speed control and the “1.2× · 7 sec ⚡ 6 sec” pop-up.Tested
hideEmbedTopBar=trueRemoves the top bar: title, owner, and the link, open and bookmark buttons.Tested
muted=1Starts the video with sound off.Documented
hide_owner=trueHides the creator's avatar before the video plays.Documented
hide_share=trueHides the share button. Covered by hideEmbedTopBar.Documented
hide_title=trueHides the video title. Covered by hideEmbedTopBar.Documented
autoplay=1Starts playing on load. Browsers only allow this when muted.Documented
t=1m20sStarts the video at a given time.Documented
  • “Tested” means checked side by side on 20 Oct 2026. The rest come from Loom's help centre.
  • The video must be set to “Anyone with the link can view” for the embed to play.
